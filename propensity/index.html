The Verdict

What each way of spending the score buys, and what it costs.

Method What it does with the score Measured here Watch out for
Outcome regression Ignores it and models the outcome instead Measured above. Everything rests on the outcome model being right.
Weighting Divides by the probability of the treatment received Measured above. A few tiny scores can carry most of the estimate.
Matching and strata Rebuilds a comparison group by hand Measured above. Coarse strata leave residual confounding inside each one.
Doubly robust Uses both models and needs only one of them Measured above. Two chances, not a guarantee: both wrong is still wrong.

The propensity score is the most useful idea in this branch and the most easily oversold.
