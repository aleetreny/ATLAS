The hyperparameter search article searched two dials of one model and found that the shape of the surface decided almost everything: four different search methods finished within a few thousandths of each other because most of the surface was already the answer. It also left a debt. The best score of a search is biased upward, and the bias grows with how much you looked.


Automated machine learning is that same search with the catalogue opened up. It picks the scaling, the dimensionality reduction, the class weighting and the model, and it reports the best score it found. This page runs the whole catalogue on four tables so that both halves of that sentence can be checked: what the search found, and what the score it reports is worth.
