Claim Status Measured here Watch out for
The attributions add up Exact, by construction Measured above. Adding up is a property of the definition, not evidence that the numbers mean anything.
The values are the right ones Exact when enumerated, approximate when sampled Measured above. The default sample sizes leave visible error on the smaller attributions.
SHAP has an answer It has two, and they differ Measured above. Which one a library computes is rarely stated and changes who gets the credit.
