The Verdict

One architecture, four measurements, and two of its constants explained.

Property What it gives you What it cost here Watch out for
Every pair one step apart No decay with distance, by construction rather than by tuning Measured above. It is paid for pairwise.
Positional encoding The only thing standing between this and a bag of words Measured above. Remove it and the model is exactly order-blind.
Dividing by the root of d Keeps the softmax out of saturation at initialisation Measured above. The damage grows with the head size.
Several heads More than one relation per layer Measured above. Not all of them are earning their keep.

What this architecture actually removed was the chain.
