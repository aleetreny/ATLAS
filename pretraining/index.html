The Verdict

Three objectives on one budget, and what each of them can and cannot do afterwards.

Objective What it can do afterwards What it scored here Watch out for
Masked Represent a sentence with both sides of every word in view. Cannot generate. Measured above. It is graded on a fraction of the tokens it reads.
Causal Generate, and represent, with only the left in view Measured above. Every representation is missing the right-hand context.
Span corruption Both, at the cost of a decoder Measured above. The same budget buys fewer layers per side.

The objective is not a detail of the training script.
