Many Series at Once

Every model so far has been fitted to one series and thrown away. Train one network across hundreds of them instead and something has to be given up, so this article measures what, and how many series it takes before the trade is worth making

Module 4.2 · Time Series · N-BEATS and Temporal Fusion

The previous article ended on a table with fifteen columns, fitted to one series. Ask the same question of a thousand series and that method fits a thousand models, none of which knows anything the others learned. Every deep forecaster of the last decade is built on the opposite bet: one network, trained on all of them, that has never seen your series before it is asked about it.
