A microphone gives you one number, thousands of times a second. Nothing in this atlas eats that. What models actually eat is a picture: a matrix of how much energy sat at each frequency during each short slice of time. The slice length is a choice, and it is the whole subject.

Thanks for reading! Every spectrogram on this page is computed in your browser, from sixteen bit samples the generator sent, with a fast Fourier transform written in assets/js/dsp.js, and checked on load against the one the generator computed in numpy. The sounds are synthetic on purpose: a source and a filter whose parameters were written down first, so the pitch, the resonances and the boundaries are known exactly rather than annotated. Everything was measured by src/utils/generate_spectrograms_data.py.
