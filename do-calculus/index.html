The Verdict

What the graph decides, what it cannot decide, and what changes when the question stops being about an average.

Question How it gets answered Measured here Watch out for
What do I adjust for? A graphical criterion, checked against exact arithmetic Measured above. Adjusting for everything available is a different rule, and it is not the same one.
What if there is no valid set? Two steps through a mediator instead of one step around Measured above. It needs its own assumptions, and they are as untestable as the others.
Who does it help? A forest that splits on the effect rather than on the outcome Measured above. Without residualising first, the same forest inherits the confounding.
Is any of this testable? The graph is an input; the arithmetic on top of it is not in doubt Measured above. Everything on this page is exact given the graph, and the graph is still a claim about the world.

This article can compute things the other three could only assume.
