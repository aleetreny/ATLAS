Module 3.1 · Adversarial · Counterfeit

Everything in the first two sections of this site was handed some data and asked a question about it. Predict this column, split these points, find the axes. Even the autoencoder, which had to put an image back together, was only ever asked to reproduce something it had already been shown.


This one is asked for a picture nobody took. Not a compressed copy of one in the training set: a new sample from the distribution the training set came out of. And the trouble is immediate, because there is no loss for that. You cannot write down how wrong a picture that nobody has ever seen is, since the thing it is supposed to be near is a distribution rather than an answer.


The adversarial answer is to stop trying to write the loss down and hire one instead. Train a second network whose whole job is to tell the real samples from the produced ones, and let the first network's loss be whatever makes the second one wrong. The loss is now a player, and it moves back.

A Classifier Standing In For A Distance

The two players, written down. The generator turns noise into samples and the discriminator scores them, and the value they are fighting over is:

\( \min_G \max_D \; V(D, G) = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{\text{data}}}\big[\log D(x)\big] + \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p_z}\big[\log\big(1 - D(G(z))\big)\big] \)

For any fixed generator, the inner maximisation has a closed form answer, and it is worth seeing that it is not a network at all but a ratio of densities:

\( D^{*}(x) = \dfrac{p_{\text{data}}(x)}{p_{\text{data}}(x) + p_{g}(x)}, \qquad V(D^{*}, G) = 2\,\mathrm{JSD}(p_{\text{data}} \,\|\, p_g) - 2\log 2 \)

So the discriminator is a divergence with a training loop wrapped around it. Below, on a mixture of two gaussians with a single gaussian trying to match it, all of that is integrated on a grid rather than quoted.

The data

An even mixture of two gaussians. It has two modes, and nothing in the model family below has two of anything.

A model that cannot be right

One gaussian, two parameters. There is no setting of them that equals the data, so every objective on this page has to choose how to be wrong, and they choose differently.

The best discriminator there is

Not trained: computed. \(D^{*}\) is one where only the data has mass, zero where only the model does, and one half where they are equally likely. No amount of architecture beats this, because it is the answer.

Which makes the game a divergence

Put \(D^{*}\) back into \(V\) and the Jensen-Shannon divergence falls out. Training a discriminator well is estimating a distance; training it badly is estimating it badly.

Which is the argument for replacing the classifier with a critic: drop the sigmoid, ask for a function that is 1-Lipschitz instead of one that is bounded, and the quantity it estimates becomes the earth mover's distance rather than a divergence that saturates.

\( W(p_{\text{data}}, p_g) = \sup_{\|f\|_L \le 1} \; \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{\text{data}}}[f(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_g}[f(x)] \)

The two ways to enforce that constraint are the two WGAN papers: clip every weight into a small box, or penalise the critic when the norm of its input gradient leaves 1. Both appear in the runs below, and neither of them enforces anything exactly.

The Verdict

What each idea on this page is worth, at this scale, on these datasets.

Idea What it buys Measured here Watch out for
A learned loss An objective for "make something new" where no hand written one exists Measured above. The loss is only as good as the opponent, and the opponent is itself a fit. A number going down here does not mean what it means anywhere else on this site.
The non-saturating loss A usable gradient in exactly the regime where the original one has none Measured above. It is not the divergence the theory analyses, so the neat statement about Jensen-Shannon describes a loss nobody trains with.
A Wasserstein critic A distance that still has a slope when the two distributions do not overlap Measured above. Neither clipping nor the gradient penalty actually delivers a 1-Lipschitz function, and the critic needs several updates per generator step, so an equal number of generator updates is not an equal amount of arithmetic.
Convolutions in both players Weight sharing on a signal that is translation structured, in the generator as well as the classifier Measured above. The DCGAN recipe is a list of things that stopped a 2015 training run diverging, not a theorem. Several of its rules are worth less than the seed.
A calibrated yardstick Being able to say a generator improved without pointing at a picture Measured above. Every automatic score is a distance in some network's feature space, and it inherits that network's opinions. Calibrate it against a floor and a ceiling or it is decoration.
