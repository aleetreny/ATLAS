References + Open Source


[1] Linear Programming and Extensions
(Dantzig, G. B., 1963; the simplex method, and the rule that this page pushes off a cliff)

[2] How Good Is the Simplex Algorithm?
(Klee, V. and Minty, G. J., 1972; the deformed cube whose every corner the steepest rule visits, reproduced here to the pivot)

[3] A New Polynomial-Time Algorithm for Linear Programming
(Karmarkar, N., 1984; the interior point method, which crosses the polytope instead of walking its edges)

[4] An Automatic Method of Solving Discrete Programming Problems
(Land, A. H. and Doig, A. G., 1960; branch and bound, counted here in nodes with the bound switched on and off)

[5] The Hungarian Method for the Assignment Problem
(Kuhn, H. W., 1955; the structure that makes a relaxation come out whole on its own)

[6] HiGHS
(Huangfu, Q. and Hall, J. A. J.; the open solver behind scipy.optimize.linprog and milp, which every number here is checked against)
