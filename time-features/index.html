A Forecast Is a Table

Write the past into columns and the future into a target and any regressor in this atlas can forecast, which is what most people who forecast for a living now do, and which breaks in three ways that a model built for series cannot break in

aleetreny

Module 4.2 · Time Series · Feature Based Forecasting

The two articles before this one wrote down a shape for the series and estimated a handful of numbers inside it. There is another way, and it is the one that wins competitions: put the series in a table. One row per month, the target is that month's value, and the columns are anything you can compute from before it. Then reach for the gradient boosting from module 1 and stop thinking about time altogether.
