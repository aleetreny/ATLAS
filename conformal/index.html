The Verdict

What the guarantee covers, what it does not, and what each repair costs.

Claim Status Measured here Watch out for
Works for any model True, and not approximately Measured above. A bad model gives valid intervals that are too wide to act on.
Exact in finite samples True, with a formula Measured above. It is exact on average over calibration sets, and yours is one draw.
Ninety percent coverage True marginally, false for any group you care about Measured above. The average hides a group above and a group below.
Assumption free False Measured above. Exchangeability is one assumption, and time series break it by construction.
