Style

A latent space nobody asked anything of, and what happens when you do: where the noise enters, what shape it is allowed to have, and which layer ends up holding which idea

aleetreny

Module 3.1 · Generative AI · Adversarial

The previous article ended with a generator whose latent space worked: a straight line between two noise vectors passed through digits the whole way, so the map from noise to picture was smooth. That is all it had. No coordinate meant anything, nothing stopped one number from controlling stroke width and slant and which digit it was all at once, and there was no way to change the thickness of a seven without risking its identity.


StyleGAN's answer is two changes, and they sound like plumbing. Put a small network in front of the latent, and stop feeding the latent in at the input: feed it into every layer, as a pair of numbers per channel that say what statistics that layer's output should have. Both changes are about where the latent enters, and the claim is that where it enters decides whether the space has any structure.

Where The Latent Enters

The two networks below have almost the same weight count and are trained on the same sprites with the same loss for the same number of steps. The only difference is the route the latent takes to the pixels.

The ordinary way

The noise vector goes through one dense layer into a small feature map, and everything after that is convolution. Whatever structure the latent space ends up with is a side effect.

A network in front of it

Four layers between the noise and anything that draws. The point is not capacity, it is that \(w\) need not be gaussian: the mapping is allowed to do whatever bending the data demands, so the synthesis network does not have to.

A style per block

The input is now a learned constant, and \(w\) arrives at every block instead, as a scale and a shift applied after each block normalises its own channels. Each block gets its own copy, which is what makes mixing two samples possible at all.

And a page of noise

One noise map per block, scaled by a learned amount per channel. The bet is that a generator given a free source of randomness will spend it on the details that do not need remembering, and leave the style to carry what does.

Measured below.

\( \mathrm{AdaIN}(x_i, y) = y_{s,i}\,\dfrac{x_i - \mu(x_i)}{\sigma(x_i)} + y_{b,i} \)

Per channel \(i\), per sample: throw away the statistics this block produced and adopt the ones the style asks for. The style is an affine map of \(w\), so what the latent controls is not the content of a feature map but its scale and offset, one number each, per channel, per block.

The Verdict

What each idea on this page is worth, at this scale, on this dataset.

Idea What it buys Measured here Watch out for
A mapping network A latent whose distribution is allowed to match the data's shape instead of being forced to stay gaussian Measured above. It only helps where the data's factors are entangled. On a dataset with an independent, unbounded set of factors there is nothing for the mapping to unbend.
Styles per block Control that is localised by scale, so an edit can be made at one resolution and not the others Measured above. Which factor lands in which block is decided by the training run, not by the architecture. The layers are ordered by resolution, and what that ordering buys depends entirely on whether the data's factors are ordered by scale too.
Per-pixel noise A free source of randomness for the parts of an image that carry no information Measured above. Free randomness is only free if the model would otherwise have spent latent capacity on it. Measure what it changes before believing it changed the right thing.
Truncation A dial between typical samples and varied ones, after training, at no cost Measured above. It is a way of hiding the tails of the model, and the tails are where the failures live. A sample sheet at a low setting is not evidence about the model, it is evidence about its middle.
