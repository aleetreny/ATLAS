The Verdict

What an instrument buys, what it costs, and which of the two is checkable.

Requirement What it does Measured here Can the data check it?
Relevance The instrument has to move the treatment Measured above. Yes, and this is the one everybody reports.
Exclusion It must not touch the outcome any other way Measured above. No, not with one instrument.
Independence Nothing hidden may cause both the instrument and the outcome Measured above. No, and it is the same kind of claim the first article ended on.
Monotonicity Nobody does the opposite of what the lever pushes them to Measured above. No, and without it the answer is not an average of anything.

Three articles in, the pattern is hard to miss.
