Running the diffusion in a small learned space instead of in pixels costs a fortieth of the network; the price is a limit on quality that is fixed before any diffusion is trained, and almost nobody reports it

aleetreny

Module 3.3 · Diffusion · Latent Diffusion

The previous article priced diffusion in pixel space and the answer was uncomfortable. On fourteen by fourteen digits, getting the samples close enough to real ones to be worth quoting took three quarters of a million weights, seventy seven times what the same process needs on a two dimensional toy. Most of that network is not learning what a digit is. It is learning that neighbouring pixels are similar.


So do that part once, with an autoencoder, and run the diffusion in the small space it produces. Decode at the end. This is the idea behind every image generator you have used, and the saving it buys is enormous and easy to measure.
