Module 4.3 · Audio · WaveNet and Whisper

The previous article turned sound into a picture and stopped there, because that is where most audio pipelines stop. The two models that changed what audio could do go in opposite directions from it. One throws the picture away and works on the samples themselves, generating them one at a time. The other keeps the picture, never looks at a waveform again, and writes down words.
