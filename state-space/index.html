Attention bought a path of length one between any two positions, and the bill for it grows with the square of the length.


The way out starts by noticing which part of a recurrence caused the trouble.

$$h_t = a \odot h_{t-1} + b\, x_t, \qquad y_t = c^{\top} h_t \qquad \Longleftrightarrow \qquad y = k * x, \quad k_t = c^{\top}(a^{t} \odot b)$$

Those two are the same function.
