A Model That Never Saw Your Data

Turn numbers into letters, train a language model on a pile of series, and then ask it to finish a sentence it has never read. The idea is absurd until it works, and the only way to know whether it worked is to be certain about what was in the pile

Module 4.2 · Time Series · Pretrained Forecasters

The previous article ended with a network trained on hundreds of series and asked about a real one it had never seen. This one takes that seriously. The recipe is the one Chronos describes and it is startling in its plainness: scale a window, chop the values into a fixed alphabet, and train a small transformer to predict the next letter. No fitting to your series, no parameters estimated from it, no assumption about its shape. Just a continuation.
