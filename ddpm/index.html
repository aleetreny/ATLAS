Module 3.3 · Diffusion · Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

Add a little gaussian noise to a picture. Add a little more. Keep going for a thousand steps and what you have left is indistinguishable from static: whatever the picture was, it is gone. Nothing about that is clever, and that is the point, because every single step of it is a gaussian and gaussians are the one thing everybody can compute with.


The model is the process run backwards. Not in one leap, which would be the impossible problem again, but one small step at a time: given something slightly noisy, guess what it looked like slightly less noisy. That is a regression, it has a squared error loss, and there is nothing variational or adversarial about it.
